Import from Excel

Upload .xlsx, .xls or .csv. Both layouts work: one row per wedding with tasks listed in one cell (one per line), or one row per task. Lines like "MUA (couple secured - Diana)" or "Content creator (not required)" are read automatically.

Paste my task update

Paste your list as you'd send it on WhatsApp. Each wedding starts with a line like "📅 10 Oct 2026 — Aaron & Yin Wen", then one task per line. ✅ marks a task done, 🆗 in progress, ❌ not started yet. "not required" and "ASAP" are understood, and a date and time like "30/Sep 8pm" becomes a reminder. Every task pasted is assigned to you.
